# Twitch Helix Integration

The **Twitch** tab uses the modern **Twitch Helix API** (replacing Snaz's legacy v3/Kraken integration) to export live viewer counts and update your stream title and category directly from OSMV.

---

## Authentication Setup

1. Create or open an application in the [Twitch Developer Console](https://dev.twitch.tv/console/apps) with an OAuth Redirect URL of `http://localhost`.
2. Enter your **Twitch Client ID** and **Channel username** in the **Twitch** tab.
3. Click **Get OAuth Token** to open Twitch authorization in your browser (requests the `channel:manage:broadcast` scope).
4. OSMV starts a temporary local listener on `http://localhost` (port `80`, `180` seconds timeout) and fills and saves your **OAuth Access Token** automatically once you approve access.
5. If port `80` is occupied or blocked on your machine, copy the redirect URL from your browser address bar and paste it into the **OAuth Access Token** field (OSMV extracts `#access_token=...` automatically).

---

## Live Viewer Count (`TwitchViewers.txt`)

Polls `GET /helix/streams` on the configurable interval (minimum 15 seconds, default 60 seconds) and writes `shared/dynamic_text/TwitchViewers.txt`.

- `$viewers`: Current live viewer count
- **Offline text**: Custom message written when the channel is offline (default: `Offline`)
- Example format: `Viewers: $viewers` -> `Viewers: 1423`

---

## Stream Title, Game Search & Favorites

- Search live Twitch categories via `GET /helix/search/categories`.
- Select a category and save it to your **Favorite Games (Presets)** for one-click switching (existing Snaz `games-custom.json` files in `shared/` are also imported automatically if presets are empty).
- Click **Update Stream** to apply your title and selected game immediately via `PATCH /helix/channels`.
