# Discord Rich Presence (RPC)

OSMV includes a pure C++20 **Discord IPC** client (`\\.\pipe\discord-ipc-0..9` on Windows and `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/discord-ipc-0..9`, Flatpak, Snap, and `/tmp` sockets on Linux) with zero external RPC library dependencies.

---

## Key Features

- **Listening Activity (`type: 2`)**: When music is playing, OSMV displays a **Listening** activity (`status_display_type: 1`) showing the track title on line 1, `by Artist` on line 2, and the album name on line 3 only when available (without duplicating the artist name).
- **Automated Album Artwork**: Queries the iTunes Search API for 500x500 artwork, strips YouTube `" - Topic"` channel suffixes when searching, verifies artist matching to avoid mismatched covers, caches up to 32 recent lookups (LRU), and falls back to HTTPS `art_url` or your configured `large_image_key`.
- **Live Health & Error Detection**: Monitors pipe status continuously, verifies `SET_ACTIVITY` replies from Discord (surfacing errors cleanly in the GUI status pill), and uses UTF-8-safe 128-byte truncation.
- **Custom Idle Status**: Displays your custom `details`, `state`, and idle Art Assets when no music is playing or when music integration is unchecked.

## How to Setup

1. Open the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications) and create an Application (e.g., `OSMV`).
2. Copy the **Application ID** (Client ID) and paste it into **Application Client ID** in the **Discord RPC** tab.
3. (Optional) Under **Rich Presence -> Art Assets**, upload images matching your configured keys:
   - Fallback cover key (e.g., `placeholder` or `osmv_logo`)
   - Playback status badges: `playing`, `paused`, `stopped`
4. Enable **Discord RPC**; OSMV connects automatically and persists your configuration.

## Troubleshooting Visibility

If OSMV displays **Connected to Discord** but nothing appears on your profile:
- Ensure your Discord status is not set to **Invisible** (Discord hides Rich Presence while Invisible).
- Check Discord **User Settings -> Activity Privacy** and make sure **Share my detected activities with others** is enabled.
- Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner of Discord to inspect your full activity card.
